📷 Profile Photo Guidelines

Photo upload se pehle ye guidelines zaroor padhein:

  • Photo simple aur normal look mein honi chahiye.
  • College uniform mein clear passport-size photo upload karein.
  • No filter, beauty effect, edited look ya unusual pose.
  • Kisi other costume/dress mein photo upload na karein.
  • Photo clear, front-facing aur proper lighting mein honi chahiye.
  • Photo ek baar upload hone ke baad dobara upload nahi ki ja sakti.
⚠️ Agar inappropriate/non-compliant photo upload ki gayi to ₹500 fine liya jayega.
